# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, respecting differing
viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for
the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language
or imagery, personal or political attacks, trolling, and publishing another
person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful. Reports may be sent privately through the
repository's security reporting channel when confidentiality is required.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
